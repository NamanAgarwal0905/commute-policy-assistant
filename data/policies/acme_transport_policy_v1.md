# Acme Technologies - Employee Transport Policy

Company: Acme Technologies
Policy version: v1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Policy owner: Acme HR and Admin

## 1. Eligibility

Employees in grades L1 to L6 are eligible for office transport if their home is within 35 km of the office. Employees whose shift ends after 8 PM or starts before 6 AM are eligible regardless of grade. Contractors are not eligible.

## 2. Night Shift and Late Drops

Women employees who are dropped home after 8 PM must be accompanied by a security escort. The transport desk assigns the escort automatically when the drop is rostered. Drops before 8 PM do not need an escort.

## 3. Booking Cut-off Times

A cab must be booked at least 4 hours before the pickup time. A booking can be cancelled up to 2 hours before pickup without penalty.

## 4. Cost Caps

Rostered shift trips are free for the employee. Ad-hoc trips are paid by the company up to INR 1,500 per employee per month. Anything above the cap is paid by the employee.

## 5. Ad-hoc Trips and Approvals

An ad-hoc trip is any trip that is not on the employee's roster. Every ad-hoc trip needs approval from the employee's reporting manager. An employee may take at most 4 ad-hoc trips per month.

## 6. Weekend and Holiday Travel

Saturday pickups and drops are allowed only with prior manager approval. Sunday and public holiday travel is not provided, except for approved critical support shifts.

## 7. Guest Travel and Airport Trips

Airport trips need approval from the transport desk at least 48 hours before the trip. Visitors and guests may travel in company cabs only with written approval from the Admin team.

## 8. No-show and Cancellation

A no-show means missing a booked pickup without cancelling. The first no-show in a calendar month gets a written warning. A second no-show in the same calendar month suspends the employee's cab access for 7 days.

## 9. Notes

Lost property must be reported to the transport desk within 24 hours.
