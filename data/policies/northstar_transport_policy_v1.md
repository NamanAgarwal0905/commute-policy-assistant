# Northstar Finance - Commute and Transport Policy

Company: Northstar Finance
Policy version: v1
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Policy owner: Northstar People Operations

## 1. Eligibility

Employees in grade M2 and above are eligible for office transport if their home is within 25 km of the office. Employees on shifts that end between 9 PM and 6 AM are eligible regardless of grade.

## 2. Night Shift and Late Drops

Women employees who are dropped home after 10 PM must be accompanied by a security escort. Drops between 8 PM and 10 PM do not need an escort. The escort is arranged by the vendor and confirmed by the transport desk.

## 3. Booking Cut-off Times

A cab must be booked at least 12 hours before the pickup time. A booking can be cancelled up to 4 hours before pickup without penalty.

## 4. Cost Caps

Rostered shift trips are free for the employee. Ad-hoc trips are paid by the company up to INR 2,500 per employee per month. Anything above the cap is paid by the employee.

## 5. Ad-hoc Trips and Approvals

An ad-hoc trip is any trip that is not on the employee's roster. Every ad-hoc trip needs approval from the employee's department head. An employee may take at most 6 ad-hoc trips per month.

## 6. Weekend and Holiday Travel

Saturday pickups are permitted for eligible shift employees with 24 hours' notice. No special approval is needed. Sunday and public holiday travel needs approval from the department head.

## 7. Guest Travel and Airport Trips

Airport trips need approval from the department head at least 24 hours before the trip. Guests are not allowed in company cabs.

## 8. No-show and Cancellation

A no-show means missing a booked pickup without cancelling. The first no-show in a calendar month is charged a fee of INR 200. A second no-show in the same calendar month is charged INR 500 and the employee's manager is informed.
