# Orbit Retail - Store Staff Transport Policy

Company: Orbit Retail
Policy version: v1
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Policy owner: Orbit Retail Operations

## 1. Eligibility

All store and warehouse staff on a scheduled operations shift are eligible for transport if their home is within 20 km of the work location. Head office staff are not eligible.

## 2. Night Shift and Late Drops

Women employees who are dropped home after 9 PM must be accompanied by a security escort. Drops before 9 PM do not need an escort.

## 3. Booking Cut-off Times

A cab must be booked at least 2 hours before the pickup time. A booking can be cancelled up to 1 hour before pickup without penalty.

## 4. Cost Caps

Rostered shift trips are free for the employee. There is no monthly allowance for ad-hoc trips; an employee pays INR 50 per ad-hoc trip.

## 5. Ad-hoc Trips and Approvals

An ad-hoc trip is any trip that is not on the employee's roster. Every ad-hoc trip needs approval from the store manager.

## 6. Weekend and Holiday Travel

Weekend and holiday travel is restricted to employees on scheduled operations shifts. Employees who are not on an operations shift cannot book a cab on weekends or holidays.

## 7. Guest Travel

Guests may not travel in company cabs.

## 8. No-show and Cancellation

A no-show means missing a booked pickup without cancelling. Three no-shows in a calendar month suspend the employee's cab access for 7 days.
